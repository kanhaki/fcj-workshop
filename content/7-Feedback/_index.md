### Overall Experience at FCAJ

**1. Working Environment**  
My time at the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) has been incredibly rewarding. The environment strikes a great balance between professional rigor and community support. Whenever I hit a roadblock with my code or AWS configurations, there was always someone willing to jump in and brainstorm solutions with me.

**2. Mentor & Admin Support**  
I want to express my deep appreciation for the mentors. Rather than just giving me the answers, they guided me on how to read AWS documentation properly and debug complex issues like Cognito token validation and React state updates. The admin team was also fantastic in organizing workshops and ensuring our workflow was seamless.

**3. Alignment with My Studies**  
The work on the **Startups Blogs** project perfectly bridged the gap between the theoretical Computer Science concepts I learned at Saigon University and real-world software engineering. Building a full-stack application from scratch made the textbook knowledge come alive.

**4. Technical Growth**  
Over these 8 weeks, I’ve grown from having basic web development knowledge to confidently deploying and integrating cloud services. Mastering NestJS, Prisma, and especially Amazon Cognito has been the highlight of my technical development during this internship.

**5. Team Culture**  
I really enjoyed the collaborative atmosphere. Team members weren't just focused on their own tasks; we frequently reviewed each other's code, shared learning resources, and celebrated small wins together. 

**6. Internship Support**  
The flexible working schedule and the continuous stream of internal technical workshops provided immense value. It allowed me to balance deep-focus coding time with continuous learning.


### Key Highlights

- **The Best Part**: Seeing my authentication flow actually work in a live environment after days of debugging! The direct mentorship from experienced cloud engineers was invaluable.
- **Areas for Improvement**: I would love to see even more hands-on "Tech Talk" sessions, perhaps focusing on advanced CI/CD pipelines or serverless architectures, as these topics were highly discussed during our Hackathon events.
- **Recommendation**: I will absolutely recommend the FCAJ program to my juniors at Saigon University. It’s a fantastic launchpad for anyone serious about Cloud Computing and Web Development.


### Final Thoughts

- **Future Outlook**: I am eager to apply everything I’ve learned here to my upcoming university projects and, hopefully, continue contributing to the cloud engineering community.
- **Acknowledgments**: A sincere thank you to the FCAJ mentors, the admin team, and my fellow interns for making this 8-week journey such a memorable and impactful experience!
